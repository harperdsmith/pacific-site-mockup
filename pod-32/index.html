Pod 32256 NVIDIA B300s in a 40 foot container

WHAT IT IS

A complete data center in a 40 foot high-cube container, with power distribution, direct-to-chip liquid cooling and 32 nodes of NVIDIA B300s, 8 to a rack across 4 racks, all in one box. Power and cooling are built in and tested at the factory before it leaves. One customer gets the whole machine as single-tenant bare metal.

HOW IT LANDS

It arrives on one truck, is dropped onto a powered pad, and switches on within days once the pad is live. Under 1 MW, so it runs on a connection that already exists. Put one on a stalled site and it earns while the big build catches up, then it leaves on the same truck it arrived on.

ONE POD OR MANY

The pod is the building block. One pod is a single megawatt site, and several clustered together form a campus, with capacity growing where and when the power becomes available.

FABRIC, POWER AND COOLING

The 400G fabric is offered in two configurations, 3.2 or 6.4 Tbps per node. Power is fed on A and B paths throughout, and the cooling runs direct to chip, from cold plates through rack manifolds to the coolant distribution units, carrying the heat straight off the silicon.
