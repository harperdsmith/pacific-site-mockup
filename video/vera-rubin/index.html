Vera RubinThe next configuration, in development

THE NEXT PLATFORM

NVIDIA has named Rubin as the generation after Blackwell. Pacific is designing the next configuration of the pod around it, and the work is under way now, alongside Pod 32.

SAME CONTAINER

The intent is the same container, with the power and cooling inside reworked for the new platform. A data center design now has to carry more than one generation of GPU, and the aim is that the site and the connection stay put while the compute inside moves on.

STATUS

The Vera Rubin configuration is in development and there is no specification to publish yet. Pod 32, with NVIDIA B300s, is the current configuration. If you are planning around Rubin, book a call.
