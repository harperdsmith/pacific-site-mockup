The data center factory

Pacific manufactures self-contained, portable AI data centers at scale.

This is the fastest path to bringing compute online. We deliver functional data centers to sites where the power is already developed, and they generate tokens within days.

Pods can be single megawatt sites or clustered to form hyperscale campuses.
