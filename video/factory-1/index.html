Factory 1Factory and R&D shop, San Francisco. Opening early next year.

WHY A FACTORY

SpaceX did not just build rockets, it built rocket factories. Data centers today are built the way buildings are built: bespoke, on site, over years, by trades that are running out. SemiAnalysis puts electricians alone at about a third of the hours on a data center site. Pacific's factory moves those hours indoors.

POD 1

Pod 1 will be built here first, in phases: shell and electrical, then cooling and plumbing, then compute, then GPUs. It is there to prove the engineering, the shop, the contractors and the supply chain. A production line follows, eventually run with robots.

LONGEST LEAD PARTS

The supply chain is the other half. It sat idle for twenty years before AI arrived. So Pacific is bringing the longest-lead parts in house, starting with switchgear and the transformer skid, with research under way into high-voltage transformers.
